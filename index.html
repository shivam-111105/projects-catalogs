Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about kits, delivery, and college project support.

Can I get a kit delivered to my college campus today?
Yes! If the kit is assembled and in stock, we regularly arrange same-day handoffs in Noida and Greater Noida college campuses (or nearby metro stations) through WhatsApp coordination.
Will you help me understand the circuit and code for my viva?
Absolutely. Every kit comes with thoroughly commented code. When you pick it up, we can walk you through the logic, circuit connections, and common questions professors ask in lab vivas.
Can you build a custom project that is not in the catalog?
Yes! Tap the "Custom Build" card or the button below. Send your project problem statement or circuit diagram, and we'll quote components and build time.
What if a sensor stops responding during my college presentation?
Every kit is rigorously tested before delivery. If you encounter any issues connecting it to your laptop or COM port, you can WhatsApp us anytime for quick troubleshooting support.

Have your own idea? Get it built.

Share your synopsis, circuit schematic, or rough idea. We source components, solder the board, and write clean test code.
